Welcome to Pinky Blush Makeover

A Beauty Parlour in Kadachanendhal, Madurai, Built Around You

Pinky Blush Makeover is a premium beauty parlour in Kadachanendhal, Madurai, created for women who want more than a quick service — a calm, hygienic space where every treatment is planned around their skin, their hair and their occasion. Located on Narasingam Main Road, opposite Ammal Medical, our studio brings together bridal makeup artistry, hair styling and everyday skin and hair care under one roof.
